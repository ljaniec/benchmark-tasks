# Apply threadlocker around a machinery mounting bolt

Local prototype, not submission-ready. Full-score robot feasibility and physical transfer calibration remain open.

The selected operation is application of medium-strength semi-solid threadlocker around the engagement threads before reassembly. [Henkel's instructions](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/industrial-adhesives/central-pdp.html/loctite-248/BP000000153639/variation/462476.html) specify a circumferential band and material in the thread roots. Curing and subsequent tightening are outside this task's current scope. The [technical data sheet](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-248-en_GL.pdf) supports the wax-like contact application, but does not establish our provisional transfer coefficient.

No direct threadlocker task was found in the 4087-task board snapshot checked on 2026-11-20. Accepted thread-seal tape, grease-gun application and painting tasks remain relevant overlap checks; novelty is not guaranteed by the material name.

The bolt is actual-size M16 x 80 mm, 2 mm pitch, 24 mm head flats and 10 mm head height. The thread uses 60-degree flanks, a truncated crest and root. The active first 16 mm has separate convex helical contact segments; uncoated thread regions use a cylindrical collision envelope under the same rendered thread. The wax plug is 10 x 10 x 8 mm in a 80 x 24 x 24 mm rectangular carrier, so the handling geometry is reproducible instead of depending on unmeasured commercial packaging. Its 8 mm projection provides carrier clearance during application. A fresh plug would be cut from semi-solid material during reset. This tool adaptation and its transfer law need a physical check before submission.

Only native wax-to-bolt contacts with tangential sliding can transfer material. Source volume decreases by exactly deposited volume. The local smear kernel extends at most one thread pitch axially and 12 degrees to either side of a contact; it cannot paint remote regions. The film is rendered on the thread, and the evaluator separately checks root coverage. A positional proximity event alone cannot create coating. Material chemistry, cured bond strength and instant curing are not modeled.

Run with the pinned environment in `DEPENDENCIES.json`: set `ISAAC_PYTHON`, `INSPECT_OVERLAY`, `ARENA_ROOT`; then `bash run_inspect.sh --policy hold --steps 200 --output RUN_SMOKE --check-reset`. Run CPU checks with `python -m unittest test_coating test_scorer test_dream_budget`, and independently replay scores and verify recorded policy frames with `python audit_run.py RUN_SMOKE`.

The initial 200-step reset smoke and a separate 200-step bimanual pickup rehearsal passed locally. Both objects were lifted and retained, with no invalid event; the pickup score remains 0.0 because no coating was attempted. The native contact smoke deposited material and its noncontact control deposited none. That fixture-driven material test is explicitly not robot feasibility evidence. Thirteen CPU tests pass. The pickup rehearsal took approximately 78 seconds wall time for 10 seconds simulated time, including startup; seed 0 and object jitter 0 were used.

`demo.py` now rehearses pickup and application using a fixed offline-IK action plan. It accepts only the three RGB streams and does not query object state at execution. The world evaluator is separate and may read physical state for scoring. No full-score demonstration is established yet. No paid run is warranted until a nonprivileged full-score demo, delivered-camera review and application-contract validation pass. Reset restores all poses, velocities, robot joints, camera buffers, material source and film state.

For physical judging, hold the completed bolt by its clean head and photograph the engagement band through a complete turn, with a millimetre scale and diffuse light. Judge blue material over the target three threads and their roots against the same coverage thresholds. This proposed photographic procedure still needs an independent judging trial; small policy-camera images alone are not assumed to prove 95 percent coverage. Fingers, supports and the carrier may not touch already coated target threads; native contact with visible wet film permanently invalidates the trial, and the physical judge checks the same event on video. The model does not assume that disturbed material heals or becomes dry adhesive. The task covers application only; no cured locking strength is inferred from its score.
