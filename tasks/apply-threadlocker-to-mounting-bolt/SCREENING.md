# Threadlocker application screening

Operation: prepare the engagement threads of a steel mounting bolt with medium-strength threadlocker before reassembly. The useful result is material distributed through the mating thread region, not an already cured or torque-qualified joint.

[Henkel's application instructions](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/industrial-adhesives/central-pdp.html/loctite-248/BP000000153639/variation/462476.html) support circumferential application and material in the thread roots. The [technical data sheet](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-248-en_GL.pdf) supports use of a semi-solid stick. [Henkel's threadlocking overview](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/uk/en/applications/threadlockers.html) documents industrial maintenance applications, including robots and gearboxes. These sources establish an actual operation; they do not establish an occupation-count threshold for this specific benchmark.

The board snapshot checked on 2026-11-20 contained 4,087 unique tasks. Searches included threadlocker, thread-locker, Loctite, anaerobic adhesive, and adhesive/glue/sealant with fasteners. No direct application task was found. This is a dated search result, not a reservation or proof of universal novelty.

The prototype preserves standard bolt dimensions, helical contact geometry in the application zone, finite source material, native contact, robot force limits and visible deposition. It simplifies wax flow with a local contact-transfer law, and excludes chemistry and curing. The rectangular carrier is an explicitly dimensioned tool adaptation. The bolt may remain held by its clean head at completion; arbitrary post-application placement is not tested.

Decision: continue local feasibility work. Publication remains gated on a full-score nonprivileged demonstration, defensible photographic judging and a final duplicate/importance review. Searches of other benchmark names did not produce a direct threadlocker hit, but the complete external task catalogs have not been exhaustively audited.
